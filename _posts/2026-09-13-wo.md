### 2026/09/13

#### Points $D,E,F$ are on side $AB, BC, CA$ of $\triangle{ABC}$ respectively, and $AF=BD=CE$, $\triangle{DEF}$ is equilateral. Show that $\triangle{ABC}$ is also equilateral. 

![image-20260913161605133](O:\workspace\mwo\assets\images\2026\image-20260913152140583.png)

**Prove:**

![image-20260913162606021](O:\workspace\mwo\assets\images\2026\image-20260913162515350.png)
$$
\begin{multline}
\shoveleft \text{Make }\triangle{GHI} \text{ such that points }A,B,C \text{ are on side }HI, IG, GH \text{ respectively}\\
\shoveleft \text{and }DE \parallel IG, EF \parallel GH, FD \parallel HI \implies \triangle{GHI} \text{ is also equilateral}\\
\shoveleft \text{Extend }DE \text{ and meet }HI, GH \text{ at }N,K\\
\shoveleft \text{Extend }EF \text{ and meet }IG, HI \text{ at }J,M\\
\shoveleft \text{Extend }FD \text{ and meet }IG, GH \text{ at }O,L\\
\shoveleft \implies \triangle{DON}, \triangle{ION}, \triangle{FML},\triangle{HML},\triangle{GKJ},\triangle{EKJ} \text{ are equilateral triangles}\\
\shoveleft \text{and their are congruent to each other, let their side length equls to }a\\
\shoveleft \implies LK=JO=MN=ME, \text{let its length be }t \implies DE=EF=FD=t-a \ne 0\\
\shoveleft \angle{AMF}=\angle{FLC}=60^\circ, \angle{MFA}=\angle{LCF}\implies \triangle{MFA}\sim\triangle{LCF}\\
\shoveleft \text{Similarly, }\triangle{CEK \sim \triangle{EBJ}, \triangle{BDO}\sim\triangle{DAN}}\\
\shoveleft \implies MA \cdot CL = a^2 = CK \cdot BJ = BO \cdot AN\\
\shoveleft \text{Let }MA=b \implies CL=\dfrac{a^2}{b} \implies CK=t-\dfrac{a^2}{b}\implies BJ=\dfrac{a^2}{CK}=\dfrac{a^2b}{bt-a^2}\\
\shoveleft \implies BO=t-BJ=\dfrac{bt^2-a^2t-a^2b}{bt-a^2}\implies AN\cdot BO=(t-b)\dfrac{bt^2-a^2t-a^2b}{bt-a^2}=a^2\\
\shoveleft \implies (a^2-t^2)(b^2-bt+a^2)=0 \implies b^2-bt+a^2=0\implies CK=t-\dfrac{a^2}{b}=b=MA\\
\shoveleft \implies \triangle{CEK}\cong\triangle{AFM}\implies CE=AF, \text{Similarly, }BD=CE\\
\shoveleft \implies AF=CE=BD \implies AB=BC=CA \implies \triangle{ABC} \text{ is equilateral}\blacksquare
\end{multline}
$$
